File 02 · Intercepted message

Can you crack the Culper code?

The Culper Ring swapped names and places for numbers from a secret code book. Tap each number to decode the message.

The real code book was written by Benjamin Tallmadge, Washington's spymaster, who was born right here in Setauket. Only four copies existed: Tallmadge, Washington, Abraham Woodhull and Robert Townsend each had one.

A few real codes: 711 George Washington, 721 Tallmadge, 722 Woodhull, 723 Townsend, 724 Austin Roe, 725 Caleb Brewster, 727 New York, 728 Long Island, 729 Setauket.

File 07 · Agent dossiers

Meet the Setauket spies

From 1778 to 1783, a handful of neighbors from right here ran the spy ring that fed George Washington intelligence from British-held New York. Letters traveled from the city to Setauket by horse, then across Long Island Sound by whaleboat.

721

Benjamin Tallmadge

Alias "John Bolton" · Spymaster

Born in Setauket in 1754 and a Yale friend of Nathan Hale. He organized the ring for Washington and wrote its code book.

Portrait by Ralph Earl, public domain

722

Abraham Woodhull

"Samuel Culper" · The Setauket farmer

Born in Setauket in 1750. He sent his first Culper letter on October 29, 1778, and is buried at Setauket Presbyterian Church.

Setauket Harbor, where Woodhull farmed. Photo: Russ, CC BY 2.0

725

Caleb Brewster

The whaleboat captain

Born in Setauket in 1747, he rowed spy letters across the Sound to Connecticut and was wounded in the 1782 "Spy Boat Fight."

???

Anna Smith Strong

The clothesline signal (legend)

Local tradition says a black petticoat on her line meant Brewster had arrived, and the number of handkerchiefs showed which cove. Historians call it unverifiable, but see her clothesline at the Historical Society.

Strong's Neck, the Strong family home. Photo: Russ, CC BY 2.0

724

Austin Roe

The rider

Rode about 55 miles each way between Setauket and New York City, posing as a merchant buying supplies.

Roe Tavern marker, East Setauket. Photo: DanTD, CC BY-SA 3.0

723

Robert Townsend

"Culper Jr." · Man in the city

An Oyster Bay merchant who gathered secrets in New York. His identity stayed hidden for 152 years, until 1930.

His home, Raynham Hall. Photo: Rhonda McCloughan, CC BY-SA 4.0

711

George Washington

The commander

The ring's reports helped warn of a British attack on the French at Newport in 1780.

Gilbert Stuart portrait, public domain
