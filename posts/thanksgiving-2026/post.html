The tree lights up on Main Street the night before, the bakeries are slammed by Wednesday, and by Sunday Santa is rolling through Port Jeff. Here are the traditions, local spots and events that make Thanksgiving in Three Village.

Where it all started

A harvest table on the harbor

Long before there was a Main Street, this was the home of the Setalcott people, who fished and farmed along the harbor that still carries their name. English settlers arrived in 1655, and Setauket grew up around the village green, the mill pond and the churches that still stand there today. Harvest gatherings, giving thanks for a good season on the water and in the fields, are about as old as the place itself.

This year two local programs bring that history to life. At The Long Island Museum in Stony Brook, the Boys & Girls Club of Shinnecock Nation Dance Troupe performs traditional and powwow-style dances, sharing their heritage and Indigenous culture. And the Emma S. Clark Memorial Library is hosting a talk on where our favorite Thanksgiving traditions came from, timed to the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade.

Wednesday night

Thanksgiving Eve: the lights go on in Port Jeff

Everybody's home. The college kids are back, the cousins drove in, and the night before Thanksgiving is when Port Jefferson Village lights up for the season. The Port Jefferson Business Improvement District lights the village holiday tree at the corner of the Broadways and Main Street, a short walk from dinner, a drink with old friends, or a last-minute stop for something you forgot.

The table

Cook it, order it, or let someone else do the dishes

There are three kinds of Thanksgiving hosts in Three Village: the ones who cook every dish from scratch, the ones who order the sides and take the credit, and the smart ones who book a table and skip the dishes. Our local businesses have all three covered.

Going out? Black Pearl Seafood Chophouse at Danfords in Port Jefferson is serving Thanksgiving dinner from 11 AM to 5 PM, and Mirabelle Restaurant & Tavern in Stony Brook has a Thanksgiving buffet from 11 AM to 7 PM. Both fill up, so book early.

Ordering in? Uncle Giuseppe's Marketplace in Port Jefferson Station is taking Thanksgiving catering orders through Saturday, Nov 21. For pies and desserts, Premiere Pastry Shop in Stony Brook and Rolling Pin in Setauket are the classic calls. For trays, sides and the stuff nobody wants to make, try Setauket Gourmet Delicatessen and Caterers, Pumpernickles Deli & Market, Farm to Table Catering and Cafe by Filomena or The Break Room Cafe. And don't forget the bottle for the table: Hamlet Wines & Liquors has you covered.

📞 Pro tip: call this week, not next week

Local bakeries and caterers book up fast in the week before Thanksgiving. Call ahead for pies, rolls and trays, and ask about pickup times on Wednesday, Nov 25.

Giving back

Neighbors feeding neighbors

Some of the best Three Village traditions happen in a collection basket. Every November, local groups and families quietly make sure nobody in town goes without a Thanksgiving meal.

Friday, Nov 27

Black Friday, the Three Village way

Skip the mall. In Three Village, the day after Thanksgiving means a walk through Stony Brook Village Center, where the shops roll out Black Friday deals all day, a petting zoo sets up in front of D.K. Brothers and holiday carolers stroll the village from 1:30 to 3:30 PM. Over in Port Jefferson, The Rinx at Harborfront Park opens for the season the same day, so you can work off the stuffing on the ice.

Saturday, Nov 28

Small Business Saturday: start your holiday list here

This is the day that matters most to the shops that make Three Village feel like home. Port Jefferson lists Small Business Saturday on the Village calendar, and the shops, restaurants and galleries along Main Street and East Broadway are ready for you. In Stony Brook, the village center is a holiday list in itself.

A few of our favorites: olive oils and balsamics at Amazing Olive and The Crushed Olive, chocolate at Chocology Unlimited, Swedish candy at Saturday Candy Co., gifts at Kate's Vintage Rose and Sea Creations, candles at Willow + Harlow Candle Company, local tees at The East End Shirt Company and crystals at Envision Crystal.

Sunday, Nov 29

The Sunday after: the holiday season begins

If Thanksgiving is the warm-up, the Sunday after is the kickoff. Santa rides down Main Street in Port Jefferson, the Three Village Historical Society fills the Setauket Neighborhood House with crafters and bakers, and Dickensian characters take over the Carriage Museum in Stony Brook as part of the Charles Dickens Festival.
