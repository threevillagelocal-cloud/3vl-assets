File 01 · Right in town

Pumpkin picking close to home

Our own working farm is the easiest fall win in Three Village: a few minutes from anywhere in town, no Sound Avenue traffic, and animals the little ones remember all year.

Farm · East Setauket

Benner's Farm: barnyard + pumpkin picking

56 Gnarled Hollow Rd, East Setauket · 631-689-8172

Farm animals, pumpkins and fall fun on open weekend afternoons, 12 to 4 PM: Oct 3, 4, 17 and 18 (the farm's website calendar also lists Oct 11, so check before you go). Admission is $12 for adults and $10 for kids. Little farmers can also book the Apple workshop (Sat Oct 10) or Pumpkin Fun workshop (Sat Oct 24), both 10 AM to noon.

📅 Oct 3, 4, 17, 18🕓 12-4 PM🎫 $12 adults, $10 kids

File 02 · Village festivals

Scarecrows, witches and the Harvest Festival

Both villages go all out in October. Most of it is free to walk, with small ticketed extras.

Stony Brook Village

36th Annual Scarecrow Competition

Stony Brook Village Center, 111 Main St · 631-751-2244

Scarecrows line the village all October. Pick up a free ballot in any village shop and vote for your favorite through October 25. Winners are announced at the Halloween Festival on Saturday, October 31 at 2 PM.

Free🗳 Vote Oct 3-25🏆 Winners Oct 31

Port Jefferson Scarecrow Walk

East Main Street, Port Jefferson

Dozens of scarecrows made by local businesses and groups along East Main Street, October 5 through November 2. Stroll, snap photos and vote for your favorites. Pair it with lunch in the village and a walk down to the harbor.

Village-wide, Port Jefferson

File 05 · Road trip

Worth the drive

For apple picking and giant corn mazes, head east. Plan ahead: on fall weekends Sound Avenue can crawl along at about 10 mph, with known jams at Jamesport and Laurel.

⏰ Go earlyArrive right at opening on weekends, or go on a weekday if you can.
🛣 Skip Sound Ave homeRiverhead to Mattituck can take over an hour on peak weekends. Head back before 2 PM.
🎫 Buy onlineHarbes sells weekend admission online, and some days sell out.
Riverhead · about 45 min

Harbes Orchard

5698 Sound Ave, Riverhead

Apple and pumpkin picking, the Robin Hood corn maze, kids' activities and live music 12 to 5 on weekends. Open daily: weekdays 10 to 6, weekends 9 to 6, through November 1. Harbes Farms Jamesport (1223 Route 25) adds Honeycrisp picking, a new corn maze and hayrides on weekends.

🍎 Apple picking🌽 Corn maze🎶 Live music

Quick answers

Fall in Three Village: FAQ

Where can I pick pumpkins near Setauket?Benner's Farm in East Setauket is open with farm animals and pumpkins on Oct 3, 4, 17 and 18, 12-4 PM ($12 adults, $10 kids). For a bigger farm day, Harbes Orchard in Riverhead is about 45 minutes east.
Is the Port Jefferson Harvest Festival free?Walking the festival is free. Activities like the hayride, inflatable corn maze, axe throwing and pumpkin patch are $5 each, or $45 for a 4-pass pack. It runs Oct 17-18, 12-5 PM.
When is peak fall foliage in Stony Brook and Setauket?Usually late October into early November. Frank Melville Memorial Park, West Meadow Beach and Avalon Nature Preserve are the closest leaf walks.
Are there haunted hayrides near Stony Brook?Yes. Benner's Farm in East Setauket runs Haunted Evening Hayrides Oct 9, 10, 16, 17 and 30, 6-9 PM, $17 per person. Tickets are online only, and the first hour is kid-friendly.
