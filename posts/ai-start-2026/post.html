Quick win: save an hour a day

Let AI write the first draft.

Review replies, social posts, emails, that awkward message to a customer. Free tools like ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude write a solid first draft in seconds. You just edit and send.

Works in any of these AI tools:

ChatGPTGeminiClaudeCopilotPerplexityMeta AI

The trick is telling it who you are and what you want. Pick one below, copy it, fill in the yellow parts and paste it into any AI chat.

You are the owner of [business name], a [type of business] in [town]. Write a warm, short reply to this Google review. Thank them by first name, mention one detail they shared, and invite them back. Under 60 words.

Review: [paste the review]
Write 3 short Instagram captions for [business name] in [town] announcing [this week's special]. Friendly local voice, one emoji each, end with a reason to stop in today.
Write a short, friendly email to our customers about our holiday hours: [list your hours]. Thank them for a great year. Under 100 words. Include a subject line.
A customer asked: [paste their message] Draft a polite, clear reply that explains [our policy]. Keep it kind, offer one helpful alternative, and keep it under 80 words.
✅ Always read it before you send it. AI drafts, you decide.

5

Quick win: 5 minutes, peace of mind

Set your AI house rules.

Write these on a sticky note for you and your team. They take five minutes and prevent almost every AI mistake.

🔒Protect private info

Never paste card numbers, Social Security numbers or medical details into a free AI chat. Use business tools with clear privacy rules.

🔍Check the facts

AI can sound sure and still be wrong. Double-check prices, hours and policies before anything goes out.

🤝Keep a human in the loop

AI drafts, you decide. A good chatbot hands sensitive questions to a real person.

🗣️Keep your voice

Edit drafts so they sound like you. Your customers know you, not a robot.
