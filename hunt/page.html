Contest rules

  • No purchase necessary. Visiting and scanning earns points; you never have to buy anything.
  • 1 point for each check-in. Each QR code counts once per player per day (midnight to midnight, Eastern time). Check in at as many different codes as you can find.
  • Bonus days: some spots score extra points on certain days (for example triple points). We announce them on our Facebook, Instagram and mobile app, and the check-in screen shows it.
  • You must be at the sign or sticker when you check in. Your phone's location is checked at that moment only.
  • One entry per person, with a real name, email and mobile number. Duplicate or fake entries, shared or posted codes, and automated scanning are disqualified.
  • Prize and contest dates: coming soon. We will post them here before the hunt starts.
  • Winners are confirmed by Three Village Local before prizes are given out. Our decisions are final.
  • Your name shows on the leaderboard as first name and last initial only. We never sell your information.
