How to film your clip

  • Hold your phone upright. Vertical, not sideways, framed from the chest up.
  • Face a window. Soft daylight on your face, not behind you.
  • Quiet room, phone at eye level. Prop it on books or a stand and look at the lens.
  • Use a mic if you have one. No mic? Get a little closer and speak clearly.
  • Start with your name. “Hi, I’m ___ with ___.” Then answer.
  • One clip per question. Take a couple of tries and send your favorite.
