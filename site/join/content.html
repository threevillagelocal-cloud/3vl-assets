Frequently asked questions

Is there a long-term commitment?

No. Every plan is month to month with no long-term contract. Cancel anytime, but we think you will love the results.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes, anytime. Test more exposure or scale back as your business needs change.

Who controls my business listing?

You do. You get your own login to update your info, photos and categories (based on your plan) whenever you like.

Can I get help setting up my listing?

Yes. We will guide you through setup and help with photos and content so your business looks its best.

Why choose a paid membership?

Free listings help people find you. Paid memberships help you stand out, get promoted more often and build stronger local visibility.
