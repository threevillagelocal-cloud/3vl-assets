Frequently Asked Questions


Is There a Long Term Commitment?

Nope! All of our plans are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You can cancel anytime if you feel it’s not the right fit — but we think you’ll love the results.

Can I Upgrade or Downgrade My Plan?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time based on your business needs. Whether you want to test out more exposure or scale back, we make it easy to switch.


Who Controls My Business Listing?

You! Once you're set up, you'll receive your own login credentials to access your listing dashboard. From there, you can update your business info, add photos, change categories (based on your plan), and keep your content up to date — all at your convenience.

Can I Get Help Setting Up My Listing?

Yes! Once you sign up, we’ll guide you through the setup process. If you need help creating your listing or uploading content, we're happy to assist and make sure your business looks its best.


Are Drone Photos & Videos Included?

Drone photos and video are included for VIP members and available at discounted member pricing for Getting Noticed members. We also offer drone services as a standalone option for businesses, events, and real estate.

Are Your Drone Services Professional?

Yes. Commercial aerial services are performed by an FAA Part 107 certified and insured drone operator. Availability may vary based on scheduling, location, weather, and the type of project.


Why Choose a Paid Membership?

Free listings help people find your business. Paid memberships help you stand out, get promoted more often, and build stronger local visibility across the Three Village Local platform.

Can I Use Drone Services Without Joining a Membership?

Yes. Drone services are available as a standalone service, but paid members receive the best value — with discounted pricing at Getting Noticed and included aerial content at the VIP level.
