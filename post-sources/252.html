Mark your calendar for a candlelit Thursday on Main Street. On October 15, the O'Dwyer-Brainard Team of Daniel Gale Sotheby's International Realty hosts its 4th Annual Karing for Kids fundraiser at Culpers 1778 in Setauket, with every dollar going to the Research Endowment at Stony Brook Children's Hospital.

Expect an easy, grown-up evening: refreshments, passed hors d'oeuvres, raffles, and a room full of neighbors who showed up for the same reason. It runs from 5:00 to 8:00 PM, so you can come straight from work and still be home for bedtime.

The Venue

A night at Culpers 1778

The party is at Culpers 1778, right on Main Street in Setauket. It shares its name with the Culper Spy Ring that operated here during the Revolution, so a night built around looking out for the next generation feels right at home.

Behind the Cause

A $100,000 promise, made one year at a time

Through the Daniel Gale Foundation, the team has pledged to raise $100,000 for research that directly helps kids facing serious medical challenges right here in our community. Over the past three years, with local support, they've raised more than $36,000 toward that goal. This year the aim is simple: bring more people in and close the gap.

Your Neighbors, Your Cause

Why we're sharing this one

Holly Brainard and Michael O'Dwyer are part of the Three Village Local community, and Karing for Kids is exactly the kind of thing that makes this area special: local people raising real money for a hospital that serves local families.
