MEET THE OWNER
JUDI WALLACE
Accounting & Tax Professional • Setauket Resident
Judi Wallace is the owner of JLW Accounting & Tax Services and a longtime Setauket professional serving individuals, families, nonprofits and small businesses throughout Long Island.
Clients know Judi for being knowledgeable, detail-oriented and approachable. She takes the time to explain financial matters clearly, helping people understand what is happening instead of simply handing them a stack of numbers or tax forms.
Her relationship-first approach is a big part of what makes JLW different: clients have a local professional they can call throughout the year, not just someone they hear from at tax time.
LOCAL TO THREE VILLAGE
Your Accountant Should Be Someone You Can Actually Talk To.
JLW Accounting & Tax Services is located on Route 25A in East Setauket. For Three Village residents and local business owners, that means professional tax and accounting guidance is available from someone who works and lives in the same community.
PERSONAL & BUSINESS TAX PREPARATION
Tax Season Without the Guesswork.
JLW provides tax preparation and filing support for individuals and businesses, helping clients stay on top of deadlines, documentation and filing requirements. Services include individual income tax returns, corporate returns, nonprofit returns, sales tax returns and payroll tax returns.

BOOKKEEPING SERVICES
Keep the Books Current. Keep the Business Organized.
JLW provides bookkeeping support that can include accounts payable, accounts receivable, collections, bank and credit-card reconciliations, payroll processing and compliance. The goal is to keep financial records accurate, current and useful throughout the year.
PAYROLL ASSISTANCE
One Less Thing for the Business Owner to Worry About.
Payroll support helps small businesses stay organized around employee payments, payroll processing and related tax responsibilities. Judi's goal is to help clients keep these important financial processes accurate and easier to manage throughout the year.
FINANCIAL REPORTING
Understand What the Numbers Are Telling You.
JLW helps businesses develop a clearer financial picture through accurate reporting and cash-flow insight. Better financial statements can help owners evaluate performance, forecast cash needs and make more informed business decisions.
SMALL BUSINESS ACCOUNTING
More Than a Tax Return.
JLW works with small businesses on full-cycle bookkeeping, financial statements, budgeting, cash-flow analysis, accounts receivable, accounts payable, payroll and tax preparation. The goal is to give owners reliable financial support throughout the year, not just during tax season.
NONPROFIT ACCOUNTING & TAX SUPPORT
Specialized Support for Organizations Doing Good Work.
JLW also works with nonprofit organizations, providing accounting and tax support informed by the unique reporting, funding and compliance considerations nonprofits face.
